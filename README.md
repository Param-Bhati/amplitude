## 🎧 **Amplitude — Your Server’s Residential DJ**

Amplitude isn’t a guest appearance.

*Always around. Always ready.*

## ▶️ **Start Here**

- **`/play <song>`** — Search, pick a result, and play  

**That’s it. You’re in.**

## 🎶 **Everyday Controls**

Use **slash commands** or just **click the buttons** — both work.

- **Pause / Resume** — Quick breaks  
- **Skip** — Not the mood  
- **Queue** — See what’s next  
- **Now Playing** — What’s on right now  

*No command memorizing.*  
*If you can click, you’re good.*
